## Lasting vs short-lived (patterns in this database)

Lasted 1+ year (Brookhaven, MM2, Adopt Me!, Blox Fruits, RIVALS, 99 Nights, Fish It!):
- A player economy or social layer that outlives the content: trading (MM2 knives, Adopt Me! pets, Blox Fruits fruits), or free social play (Brookhaven).
- A clear long goal or deep progression (99 nights, seas in Blox Fruits).
- Scheduled live ops: weekly updates, seasonal events, admin events. Veterans' 2026 spikes were all events (MM2 Summer, Brookhaven Friday updates).
- Format leadership: the first good version of a format keeps it (Fish It! over Hooked!, DTI over fashion clones).

Short-lived (spike then −60–90% in 1–2 months: Animal Hospital, +1 Speed games, GAG2, Anime Expeditions, The Forge, Ink Game, Dead Rails):
- Novelty-led hook with little after the first sessions (anomaly spotting, +1 speed).
- Template clones of a live leader, or a sequel that splits the original's audience.
- Hook tied to an outside moment (Squid Game season, a meme) that ends.
- Short sessions (8–12 min) with no economy to return to.

Biggest hits are merges: Steal An Egg = Steal a ___ + pets + speed stat; Plants vs Brainrots = PvZ + brainrot. Simple single-verb loops (plant, steal, run, fish) with visible progress reach the top; complexity is not required.
